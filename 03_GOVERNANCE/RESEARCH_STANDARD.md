# Premium Research Standard

When the owner says "research this properly", NEEWA creates a research manifest before concluding.

## Required evidence classes when relevant
- official product/API documentation;
- source repository/releases/issues;
- security advisories/CVEs;
- academic papers;
- independent industry analysis;
- practitioner/community experience;
- social/video source material when accessible;
- courses/public curricula where relevant;
- existing owner project evidence;
- economics/pricing;
- serious alternatives.

## Rules
- Never claim access to inaccessible content.
- Prefer primary sources for hard facts.
- Use recent sources for fast-changing products/prices.
- Separate source fact from inference.
- Record contradictory evidence.
- Record search gaps.
- Identify at least two alternatives for architecture/purchase decisions.
- Do not conclude until required source classes are satisfied or explicitly marked unavailable.

## Output
- research question;
- decision criteria;
- sources;
- evidence table;
- contradictions;
- confidence;
- recommendation;
- what would change the recommendation.
