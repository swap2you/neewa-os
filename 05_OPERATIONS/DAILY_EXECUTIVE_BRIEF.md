# Daily Executive Brief Template

## Executive summary
Three sentences maximum.

## Projects
For each active project:
- status;
- completed since last brief;
- current blocker;
- next action;
- owner action required? yes/no.

## AI operations
- total API/model spend;
- provider quota warnings;
- active/failed jobs;
- unusual retries;
- infrastructure health.

## Revenue
- leads/opportunities;
- revenue attributable;
- expected near-term actions.

## Owner decisions
Only decisions that genuinely require owner authority.

## Closed
List projects/jobs that are done and should leave active attention.
