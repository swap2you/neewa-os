# Naming Conventions

## IDs
Project: `PRJ-<SHORT>`
Job: `JOB-YYYYMMDD-###`
Decision: `DEC-YYYYMMDD-###`
Incident: `INC-YYYYMMDD-###`
Research: `RES-YYYYMMDD-###`

## Branches
`neewa/<project>/<job-id>-<slug>`

## Evidence directories
`evidence/<project>/<job-id>/`

## Reports
`<JOB-ID>_<TYPE>_<YYYYMMDD>.md`

Avoid vague names such as `final-final-v2`.
