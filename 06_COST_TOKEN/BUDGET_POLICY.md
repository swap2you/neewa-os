# Budget Policy

## 90-day owner funding ceiling
**$1,000 total** for the proving period unless explicitly changed by the owner.

## Hard principles
- budgets are enforced technically where possible;
- NEEWA cannot silently raise a budget;
- costly models are selected by task value/risk;
- retry storms are prohibited;
- Council depth scales with risk;
- GPU workers scale to zero when idle.

## Monthly target
Keep steady-state subscriptions/infrastructure around or below the owner's target unless revenue clearly justifies expansion.

## Self-funding target
By end of 90 days, target at least $300/month attributable revenue or equivalent recurring savings.

No guarantee of revenue is permitted.
