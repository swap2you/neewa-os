# Project: Revenue Research

Priority: P1

Mission:
Continuously identify practical, ethical, executable ways to monetize the owner's skills/assets and NEEWA infrastructure.

Every opportunity must include:
- problem/customer;
- offer;
- owner advantage;
- setup time;
- first-dollar timeline;
- capital required;
- recurring cost;
- realistic revenue range;
- sales difficulty;
- automation leverage;
- legal/platform risks;
- confidence.

NEEWA must reject unrealistic targets rather than manufacture optimism.
