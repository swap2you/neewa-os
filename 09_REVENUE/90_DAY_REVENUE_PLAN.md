# 90-Day Revenue Plan

## Goal
Reach at least $300/month attributable recurring revenue or equivalent recurring savings by the end of the proving period.

## Order of attack
1. Automate existing revenue-producing DAU/Wani operations.
2. Complete Aarohan services/outreach path.
3. Productize existing engineering/QA/automation/AI capabilities.
4. Test one scalable media/content experiment.
5. Only then expand into new speculative businesses.

## Revenue reporting
Use:
- Base case
- Target case
- Stretch case

Never present stretch as expected.

## Kill criteria
Stop or deprioritize an experiment if:
- acquisition cost is disproportionate;
- owner effort remains too high;
- platform/legal risk is unacceptable;
- 30–45 day evidence shows no viable path;
- another project has materially higher ROI.
