# Opportunity Scorecard

Score 1–5:
- existing owner expertise;
- customer urgency;
- willingness to pay;
- time to first dollar;
- startup cost;
- recurring cost;
- automation leverage;
- competition;
- defensibility;
- legal/platform risk;
- owner attention required.

Weighted output:
- pursue now;
- prototype;
- watch;
- reject.

Include evidence for every score above 3.
