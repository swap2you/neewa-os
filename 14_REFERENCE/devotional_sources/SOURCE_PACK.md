## SRC-01 — Ganapati Atharvashirsha (traditional hymn)

- **Class:** scripture-tradition note (not a verse-critical text in this pack)
- **Attribution:** Atharvavedic/Upanishadic hymn to Ganapati, widely recited in household and temple practice.
- **Permitted theme notes:** Ganesha as the reality behind speech, mind, and the elements of experience; invoked at the start of undertakings; associated with removal of obstacles (`vighna`) in the sense of enabling a right beginning.
- **Quotation limit:** cite this pack entry (SRC-01), not invented verse numbers.

## SRC-02 — Ganesha Purana / Mudgala Purana (puranic tradition)

- **Class:** puranic tradition note
- **Attribution:** later puranic literature devoted to Ganesha (Ganesha Purana; Mudgala Purana’s eight incarnations are a traditional listing).
- **Permitted theme notes:** Ganesha as both child-form beloved in the household and as a philosophical principle; multiple earthly descents in Mudgala listings; stories used pedagogically rather than as historical chronicle.
- **Quotation limit:** cite SRC-02; do not fabricate chapter-and-verse.

## SRC-03 — Maharashtrian public festival practice

- **Class:** living festival practice (cultural, not scripture)
- **Attribution:** common public and household observances of Ganesh Chaturthi / Vinayaka Chaturthi as practiced in Maharashtra and the diaspora, as recorded in this internal pack.
- **Permitted theme notes:** clay murti installation in the home; daily offering including modak; communal mandals; visarjan (immersion) as returning the form to water; emphasis on beginning, learning, and communal care.
- **Quotation limit:** cite SRC-03 as practice notes, not as Vedic proof.
