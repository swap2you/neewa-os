# Owner decisions

NEEWA proceeds on A0/A1 inside approved personal repos and cursor-sandbox.

Ask the owner only when:

- A2: spend, publish, production deploy, credential rotation, visibility change
- A3: live trades, bank transfers, legal identity
- a design choice changes product, cost, or irreversible outcome
- retries cannot clear a blocker

Routine button spacing, unit tests, and in-architecture refactors do not
re-open an approved design version.

Home/orb spoken voice remains a separate owner-physical check. It does not
gate Conversation orchestration.
